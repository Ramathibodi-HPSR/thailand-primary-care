In fiscal years 2024–25 the scheme grew its primary-care network by contracting more innovation units (private nurse clinics, pharmacies and medical clinics) instead of building public ones. This page tests where they went, who they reached, and how a planner would have done with the same number of sites.

3 · Then half of them left

By November 2026, units were no longer innovation units

of them are now registered as class Z, private clinics that take part in some services but are not counted as primary-care access points here. Among the 2024–25 entrants, of nurse clinics and of pharmacies left.

Most of this was not clinics closing. From 12 January 2026 the NHSO paid these units through weekly quotas from a closed budget and asked every one to re-register; units that did not could not use the new system. We read the units that left as mostly those that did not re-register. Nurse clinics could not re-register when the system opened, so part of their drop may be a delay.

Methods and limits

วิธีและข้อจำกัด

Data

  • Facilities: NHSO unit registry, snapshots of and . Primary-care units are class P plus the contracted pharmacies, nurse clinics and medical clinics (innovation units; codes R0211/R0311, R0213/R0313, R0207/R0307). Prison health units and class Z are not counted.
  • Every coordinate was checked against the unit's registered tambon; coordinates more than 2 km off were replaced by the other snapshot's, or by the population-weighted centre of the tambon (flagged).
  • Population: WorldPop 2020, 100 m, UN-adjusted, constrained ( million).
  • Roads: OpenStreetMap via Geofabrik, November 2026. Wealth: Meta Relative Wealth Index, 2.4 km tiles, 2021. Boundaries: OCHA/RTSD via HDX.

Methods

  • Distance: every populated 100 m cell to its nearest unit (straight line). Replaces the earlier ArcGIS buffer run, which it reproduces to within 1.7%.
  • Travel time: shortest paths on the road graph, with assumed speeds by road class; walking at 5 km/h on every road.
  • Equity: people ranked by wealth; concentration index; units per 100,000 people by wealth fifth.
  • Availability: Gaussian two-step floating catchment (Luo & Qi 2009; Dai 2010), 2 km, equal capacity.
  • Planner: greedy maximal covering location model (Church & ReVelle 1974), 2 km, candidate sites at the centre of uncovered people in each H3 resolution-8 hexagon.

Limits

  • Straight-line distance and assumed speeds, not observed travel. No one-way streets, turns or traffic.
  • Entry year is the start of the contract held in November 2025; units that left before then are not seen.
  • Leaving the innovation-unit classes is not closing: most units moved to class Z, likely because they did not re-register under the January 2026 rules (news reports; our reading).
  • Population is for 2020 and counts everyone, including people outside the scheme.
  • All numbers are preliminary; the working paper is in preparation.
